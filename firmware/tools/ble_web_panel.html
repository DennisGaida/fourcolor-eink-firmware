This panel only talks to what the firmware actually implements today: the 0xF000 BLE GATT service used for photo push (ble_gatt_service.cc). There is no BLE control for Wi-Fi status, memory/CPU stats, or remote reboot/cache-clear in the current firmware, so those are intentionally left out rather than faked.
